# Semiconductor customer qualification review worksheet

Tymeline publishes this manual worksheet for foundry, outsourced semiconductor assembly and test (OSAT), and test-service teams coordinating more than one customer qualification. Use it to prepare a review when multiple programs depend on the same lab, equipment or engineering time.

## Record one customer gate

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Customer program and gate | Internal program reference, qualification gate, baseline date and accountable owner |
| Acceptance criteria | Applicable customer plan or specification revision, required evidence and who can approve exceptions |
| Lot and configuration | Lot reference, device or package revision, sample disposition and links to controlled records |
| Test and capacity | Procedure revision, required setup, assigned lab or resource, planned booking and actual status |
| Result and applicability | Report reference, observation date, result and engineer assessment against the criterion |
| Dependency and recovery | Other programs competing for capacity, available options and affected dates |
| Disposition | Technical review status, evidence gaps, named approver and approval or exception reference |
| Customer sign-off | Customer acceptance status, authorized contact, approved message and recorded sign-off reference |

Review capacity across the programs using internal references. Restrict each customer-facing view to its authorized records. Use your approved qualification plan to decide what evidence and sign-off are required.

## Worked example

Two synthetic programs have approved plans requiring a chamber run. Program A has a reserved slot, but one sample lot is held for an engineering disposition. Program B can use the chamber earlier if its setup and staff are ready. Moving B forward frees no capacity for A until the team confirms when A's samples can run and checks the next available slot.

| Review question | Illustrative record |
| --- | --- |
| What can the lab confirm now? | A's booking is reserved. The lot is on hold. B's earlier slot depends on setup readiness. |
| What evidence is missing? | The authorized engineer's disposition for A's held lot and confirmation of B's setup. |
| What could change? | Booking order, evidence delivery and the customer review date. The schedule impact is unknown until both dependencies are confirmed. |
| Who decides? | The lab lead owns the capacity recommendation. The authorized technical reviewer owns the lot decision. The accountable program owner records any date change and the authorized customer contact handles sign-off. |

This example contains synthetic program and lot information. Use the worksheet to organize evidence and decisions under your own rules. The authorized reviewers determine whether a test result satisfies a customer requirement.
