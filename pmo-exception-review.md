# Engineering PMO exception review worksheet

Published by Tymeline. Use this manual worksheet to prepare a review of changed commitments, missing evidence and decisions that need an owner.

## Before the review

Record the program, the current approved plan, the report cutoff and the authorized decision makers. Link to the evidence in its approved system. Keep completed records in your controlled workspace.

| Commitment and owner | Approved date | Observation and source time | Evidence reference | Review state | Proposal or next action | Decision and approval reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | | | |

## Worked example

The dates and references below are synthetic.

| Commitment | Approved date | Observation | Review state | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Integration build, integration lead | April 12 | Build log INT-042 shows the approved revision failed its interface test on April 9. | Confirmed exception | Investigate the failure and bring recovery options to the review. |
| Qualification run, test lead | April 15 | The latest test result is missing at the report cutoff. | Evidence missing | Request the current result from the test lead. Keep the result unknown until evidence arrives. |
| Sample handoff, program lead | April 18 | PRO-008 proposes April 20 after the integration failure. | Proposal pending | The named approver reviews the evidence and records the decision. April 18 stays in the approved plan while the proposal is pending. |

For a pilot, ask the vendor to assemble the digest with read access first. Check that a user outside the approval role can raise a proposal without changing the plan. Then have the authorized owner approve or reject it and verify that the decision history retains the evidence, prior date and resulting date.

[Tymeline’s guide to exception-based program reviews](https://tymeline.ai/resources/program-review-on-exceptions) provides the workflow context. [Tymeline for PMO leads](https://tymeline.ai/pmo) describes the product’s PMO use case.
